How to use this planner

Keep it honest and specific. Add real activities first, then choose the 10 most meaningful and relevant entries.

Closed-loop workflow: Record an activity → add dates and role → note the outcome and evidence → review accuracy → mark ready → choose your final top 10 → export and cross-check against the official portal.

Your achievement list

The category choices follow the categories shown in NUS Medicine's sample CCA list. They are not a required set of ten categories; you do not need one achievement in every category.

Final submission check

The achievements list is only one part of the Medicine portfolio. Use the official instructions for your application year and qualification.
